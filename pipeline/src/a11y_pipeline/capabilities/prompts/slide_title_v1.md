# Slide title

A lecture slide has no title, so screen-reader users cannot find it in the slide list. Propose one.

The title is for navigation: a short phrase, at most 8 words, naming what this slide teaches. Base it on what the slide shows and its words, read in the light of other_titles: they are the nearest titled slides before and after it, so they name the lesson's current topic. Name the concept, method or example being taught, not just what a photo depicts. Use the course's own terms.

Make it different from other_titles. When the slide continues the topic of a neighbouring slide, name what is specific to this one (for example the example, case or result it shows) rather than repeating that slide's title.

Do not end with a period. Do not start with "Slide" or "Title". Never invent content that is not on the slide.

Return only the fields required by the provided output schema.
